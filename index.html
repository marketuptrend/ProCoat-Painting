FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from our painter clients and retail customers in Lancaster, CA.

Do you offer wholesale pricing for painting contractors? +
Yes! Register as a trade professional at our Lancaster store and receive up to 30% off retail pricing. Volume discounts apply to orders of 20+ gallons. Bring your contractor license or business card to qualify.
Can you match any paint color from another brand? +
Absolutely. Our in-store spectrophotometer can match any existing color — just bring a paint chip, sample board, or even a photo. We can match Benjamin Moore, Sherwin-Williams, Behr, and hundreds of other brands.
Do you deliver to job sites in Lancaster? +
Yes! We offer local delivery to Lancaster, Palmdale, Quartz Hill, and surrounding Antelope Valley areas. Orders over $200 qualify for free delivery. Schedule at checkout or call us directly for same-day service.
What paints do you recommend for Lancaster's hot climate? +
We recommend our Exterior Sun-Shield Coat for all Lancaster exterior projects — it's UV-resistant, heat-reflective, and rated for temperatures up to 160°F. For stucco, our Elastomeric Masonry Coating is the best choice. Come in for a free consultation!
Do you rent spray equipment? +
Yes! We rent airless sprayers (Graco, Titan), HVLP guns, and pressure washers by the day, weekend, or week. Equipment comes fully serviced and ready to use. A refundable deposit is required. Call ahead to reserve.
Are your paints California VOC compliant? +
All paints we carry meet or exceed California CARB and SCAQMD VOC regulations. We also carry a full zero-VOC line for clients who want the most environmentally friendly option or have chemical sensitivities.
